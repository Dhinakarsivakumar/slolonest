# SoloNest Android App (.APK) Package

Your Flutter Android Mobile App codebase is located at:
`d:\soloNest\solonest_mobile\`

## 📱 2 Ways to Get Your Installed Android .APK File:

### Way 1: GitHub Actions Automatic Cloud Build (Recommended & 100% Free)
1. Go to your GitHub repository: https://github.com/Dhinakarsivakumar/slolonest
2. Click the **Actions** tab at the top.
3. Select **Build Android APK** → Click **Run workflow**.
4. Once completed (~2 minutes), click **SoloNest-Android-APK** to download `app-release.apk` directly onto your PC or phone!

### Way 2: PWA Instant Mobile Installation (No Build Needed)
1. Open `https://slolonest.onrender.com/` in Mobile Chrome on your Android Phone.
2. Tap the Chrome menu (`⋮`) → select **"Install App"** / **"Add to Home Screen"**.
3. SoloNest installs instantly on your Android home screen as a native application with vibration & push notifications!
