For Guests

How do I book a room?

Browse rooms on the home page, click Book Now on any listing, choose your check-in and check-out dates, add a short message to the owner, and submit. The owner will review and approve or decline within 24 hours.

Can I book without creating an account?

No. You need a free guest account to request a booking. Sign up takes under a minute — you can also continue with Google.

What do I do if my booking is approved?

You'll receive a notification. Go to your Dashboard, open the booking, and pay securely through Razorpay. Then contact the owner directly via the in-app chat or phone.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Go to the booking detail page and click Cancel Request while the booking is still in Requested or Approved status. Once a stay is completed, it cannot be cancelled.

How do I filter rooms by city and dates?

Use the search bar at the top of the home page. Enter a city name, select check-in and check-out dates, and click Search. The results will only show rooms available for those specific dates.

For Owners

How do I list my room?

Create or switch to an Owner account in Profile Settings. Then click List Your Room in the navigation bar. Fill in the room details, set a price (per day and/or per month), add up to 5 photos, and publish.

Why does my listing show "Pending verification"?

New listings are reviewed by our team before getting a Verified badge. This usually takes under 24 hours. Submit your government ID in Profile Settings → ID Verification to speed up the process.

How do I approve or reject a booking request?

Go to your Dashboard, find the booking request, click View, and use the Approve or Reject buttons. The guest is instantly notified either way.

Can I edit or delete my listing?

Yes. On the Dashboard, click the ⋮ menu on any listing card and choose Edit or Delete. Deleting a listing also removes all its photos permanently.

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

SoloNest uses Razorpay which supports UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, and Wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, etc.).

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. SoloNest never stores your card details. All transactions are handled by Razorpay (PCI DSS compliant) with 256-bit encryption.

Are there any broker fees or hidden charges?

No. SoloNest charges no broker fees. You pay exactly what the owner lists. The owner receives the full amount.

Safety & Trust

What does "Verified" mean on a listing?

Verified listings have been reviewed by our team and the owner has submitted a government ID. It doesn't guarantee the room quality but confirms the listing is genuine.

How do I report a suspicious listing?

Open the listing detail page and click Report in the booking box. Select a reason and add details. Our team reviews all reports within 48 hours.

What should I do if I suspect a scam?

Never pay anyone outside of SoloNest's official payment page. If an owner asks for money via UPI/bank transfer before booking, report the listing immediately. SoloNest will never ask you to pay externally.
